Kit Crafting

Kit's collection of crafting recipes and custom blocks.

Chaos Crafting Table

Combine a crafting table with an amethyst shard. Each craft has a 50% chance of the normal output and a 50% chance of one completely random item. Sticks could become netherite armour; a diamond sword could become sticks.

Move the question-mark result into your inventory to reveal your reward. Includes bundled crafting recipes; special recipes such as arbitrary fireworks are not included.

More ways to craft

Alternative blast furnace and iron pickaxe recipes, plus experimental Netherite Stairs.

Horror Kids

Kit's horror mobs: Toorantula Teerantula, the Kraken boss and The Light Saver.

Two spiders. One boss.

Two giant spiders bound together with cobwebs, with red and green eyes, sixteen moving legs, and 100 health points. It attacks players and friendly or neutral mobs, leaving hostile mobs and other spiders alone.

Defeating it drops 10 netherite ingots and its spawn egg. Find Spawn Toorantula Teerantula in the Creative inventory, or use /summon horror_kids:toorantula_teerantula with cheats enabled.

Use a difficulty above Peaceful; Creative players are not attacked.

Kraken

A giant light-pink squid-inspired boss with a tapered mantle, eight curling tentacles, including two extra-long grasping arms.

600 health points (300 hearts), a named boss health bar, and tentacle attacks dealing 8 base damage plus 10 seconds of Wither. It hunts players and sea creatures in the water.

It turns and tilts as it swims, with its tentacles trailing behind. Defeating it drops 10 raw pork chops. It can keep its head above the surface while its body is in water. It begins taking land damage after 15 seconds fully out of water.

Find Spawn Kraken in Creative, or use /summon horror_kids:kraken underwater. Use a deep, open ocean and a difficulty above Peaceful. Creative players are not attacked.

Updated swimming, surface breathing and tentacle spread need an in-game retest.

The Light Saver

A yellow humanoid with red eyes and two miniature golden swords, 40% smaller than a regular zombie. It flies and lunges like a Vex, including passing through blocks.

Frail but dangerous: 8 HP (4 hearts) and 6 base damage per hit, comparable to a ghast fireball's direct impact. It attacks players and other mobs, including hostile mobs and the other Horror Kids bosses, while leaving other Light Savers alone. Creative players are not attacked.

Find Spawn The Light Saver in Creative, or use /summon horror_kids:light_saver with cheats enabled. The latest update fixes its targeting of Survival players.

Install or update

  1. Save and exit your Minecraft world. Back up important worlds before updating.
  2. Download the mod you want, then open the downloaded .mcaddon file with Minecraft Education. Repeat for any other mods you want.
  3. Wait for each import to finish. In the world's Behavior Packs, activate Kit Crafting, Horror Kids, or both. Check that the matching Resources packs are active too.
  4. Reopen your world. If an update hasn't appeared, check its active pack versions in world settings.

No GitHub account or development tools needed. Bookmark this page for future updates.
