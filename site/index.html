Toorantula Teerantula

Two giant spiders bound together with cobwebs, with red and green eyes, sixteen moving legs, and 100 health points. It attacks players and friendly or neutral mobs, leaving hostile mobs and other spiders alone. Defeating it drops 10 netherite ingots and its spawn egg.

Find Spawn Toorantula Teerantula in the Creative inventory, or use /summon kit_crafting:toorantula_teerantula with cheats enabled. Use a difficulty above Peaceful; Creative players are not attacked.

Chaos Crafting Table

Combine a crafting table with an amethyst shard to make it. Each craft has a 50% chance of the normal output and a 50% chance of one completely random item. Sticks could become netherite armour; a diamond sword could become sticks.

Move the question-mark result into your inventory to reveal your reward. Includes bundled crafting recipes; special recipes such as arbitrary fireworks are not included.

Install or update

  1. Save and exit your Minecraft world. Back up important worlds before updating.
  2. Download the pack, then open the downloaded file with Minecraft Education.
  3. Wait for the import to finish. For your first installation, activate Kit Crafting in the world's Behavior Packs and check that Kit Crafting Resources is active.
  4. Reopen your world. If an update hasn't appeared, check its active pack versions in world settings.
